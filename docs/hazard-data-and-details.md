# Hazard provenance and details

The card labels the product: local observation, official warning, institutional report, model estimate, or river-stage observation. Institutional origin is not proof that a forecast has occurred. Earthquake priority remains unchanged.

- NOAA Aviation Weather Center METAR: 55 sampled airports worldwide, five-minute refresh; the actual observation must be at most 90 minutes old. Gusts at least 70 km/h and observed thunderstorm groups become records. TEMPO/BECMG/RMK groups do not count as current observations. This is aerodrome coverage, not global city coverage.
- NWS: actual, unexpired severe thunderstorm, high wind, flash/river flood warnings in the US. A real polygon is required to put a representative marker on the map. Warnings without geometry enter Registros as regional notices without invented coordinates.
- Meteoalarm: 16 validated country feeds (listed below), every ten minutes. Regional notices without coordinates appear in Registros and Meteorologia > Ver fontes e detalhes. The list links back to Meteoalarm, credits national services and identifies forecast/expiry times. This does not cover every country.
- GDACS: existing regional flood reports keep the original start date where supplied.
- GloFAS/Copernicus through the public Open-Meteo Flood API: seven-day river-discharge context on the reverse of a flood record. m³/s, daily forecast, returned river grid coordinates; it is not street flooding or a flood probability. No local flood-stage threshold is inferred.
- Open-Meteo/WeatherAPI: modeled/aggregated conditions are labeled model estimates. They no longer play sirens, show warning toasts, become live badges, or automatically select the main card.
- ANA: the legacy series requires an explicit unit and a fresh reading. A river rise is informational, not a confirmed flood. Unspecified units are rejected. Broader ANA/CEMADEN station coverage still requires documented public access or credentials; no tokens or fictitious observations were introduced. INMET and existing cyclone-center feeds remain in use.

“Detalhes ↻” uses the existing flip, with original live DOM nodes on the reverse and an explicit return button. Information is organized in collapsible groups. Changing the event restores nodes to the front before updating them. Glass styling is preserved. Automatic population flip still runs unless the person explicitly opens the persistent detail reverse.

## Official warning expansion (October 2026)

- ECCC Canada: `https://api.weather.gc.ca/collections/weather-alerts/items?f=json&limit=500`, the official MSC GeoMet OGC collection. Five-minute refresh/cache. Up to four pages, with same-host/path validation. A missing page or truncated collection is an error, never an empty all-clear. Uses official publication/expiry/start times, region, impact and original English text. Stable region/event IDs prevent publication revisions from duplicating records. Coordinates are representative points from real polygons; advisories without verified geometry also enter Registros as regional notices. Storm-surge warnings are coastal flooding warnings, not confirmed street flooding.
- BOM Australia: seven public RSS subscriptions advertised by `https://www.bom.gov.au/rss/` cover NSW/ACT, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS and NT. Ten-minute refresh/cache, independently reported regional failures. RSS build time must be within 30 minutes. Titles and original links are syndicated with attribution, without copying full bulletins or using the restricted internal mobile API. RSS does not specify expiry, severity or geometry: these are explicitly unknown and users follow the original bulletin. Removal from the current RSS removes the notice; no artificial warning expiry or map location is invented. Notices stop displaying after 20 minutes without a valid consultation.
- Meteoalarm now covers 16 validated public Atom feeds: Germany, France, Spain, Portugal, Italy, UK, Austria, Belgium, Netherlands, Switzerland, Poland, Norway, Sweden, Finland, Denmark, Ireland. Ten-minute refresh, two parallel consultations. Test, private, cancelled and expired entries are excluded. Country/area/hazard/start-time keys collapse duplicate revisions. Only actual CAP polygons may supply map coordinates. Empty feeds mean zero notices reported by that feed, not proof that the country has no hazards. Meteoalarm/national-service attribution and CC BY 4.0 remain visible.
- NWS now retains relevant watches, warnings and advisories for flood, wind and storms, including tornadoes. Warnings without geometry are available in Meteorologia and Registros, without a map marker. Actual/expiry checks remain enforced; issued warnings and observations are never merged solely because markers are near each other.

All official warnings are listed in Meteorologia > Ver fontes e detalhes > Avisos oficiais pelo mundo. All active, timed warnings enter Registros; only warnings with verified geometry enter the map. The glass layout, earthquake selection and camera priority are preserved. Source errors remain visible; previous returned warnings are identified as such and removed when expired or without a recent consultation. Severity uses source wording, not a confidence percentage. Original text is not machine-translated or interpreted as a measured wind speed.

GloFAS/Copernicus river-discharge context already exists on flood-event details; it remains a forecast in m³/s, not a confirmed flood. Global airport METAR observations remain independently labeled as local observations. No new universal global coverage is claimed.

## Regional records and panel navigation

Record cards use “Ver no painel →” and an accessible name identifying the main panel. Official warnings additionally offer a separate “Boletim oficial ↗” link; activating it does not select the card. Cyclone records use the same panel action, where their trajectory remains available.

Regional warnings display area, institution, severity and real expiry when available. Unknown RSS validity stays explicitly unknown. Notices older than 24 hours remain visible when still present in a recently consulted source; their group says “Emitido há mais de 24h”. They never create an artificial point, distance or automatic camera focus. Manual selection opens the main panel without moving the camera or stopping earthquake waves; FOCO, Story and nearby cities are hidden for unlocated notices. The panel presents the warning facts and source bulletin instead of generic estimated impact. The mobile compact warning summary shows those facts rather than a magnitude gauge. Returning to a located event restores the original controls and presentation.
